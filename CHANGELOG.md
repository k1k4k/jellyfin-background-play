# Changelog

What's new in each version, as shown in Jellyfin's plugin catalog.

## 0.1.0

- Leaving the video player (back arrow, browser back) no longer stops the video: it keeps playing in the background.
- The same mini player bar as for music shows up at the bottom: play/pause, stop, previous/next episode, seek, volume. Click it to get back to the player.
- The next episode starts in the background without bringing the player back.
- New Stop button next to pause in the player, and a close cross at its top right, to stop the video and leave.
